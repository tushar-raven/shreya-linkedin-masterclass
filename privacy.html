Who runs this

The LinkedIn Masterclass is an independent live online event hosted by Shreya Pattar. It is not affiliated with or sponsored by LinkedIn. Questions, access or deletion requests: ORGANISER_EMAIL.

What we collect

When you register we ask for your first and last name, email address, which description fits you best, and optionally your LinkedIn profile URL and what you want from the session. We also record the page you registered from and any campaign tags in the link you used (UTM source, medium and campaign).

Why

To manage registrations for the masterclass and send you the schedule, joining link and event updates. Ticking the consent box is how you agree to those emails.

Where it is stored

Registrations are saved in a private Google Sheet run by the organiser, through Google Apps Script. Google acts as our processor. Only the organiser and people they authorise can see the data. The website itself does not set tracking cookies.

How long we keep it

We keep registration details for up to 12 months after the event, then delete them, unless you have asked to hear about future sessions.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from event emails at any time by replying "unsubscribe". You can ask to see, correct or delete your information by emailing the address above.
